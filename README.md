### Learn absolutely anything\*, mindfully

_\*Works better with STEM, though._

Mindful.ly is an AI-and-human-in-the-loop learning platform.

In a time when large language models are approaching PhD-level intelligence, Mindful.ly is an AI-enabled learning platform that reconciles the gap between human and AI. The team behind Mindful.ly believes that AI should not replace humans, but help in their enhancement; just as well as a book is a tool to safekeep knowledge, so is AI—a tool that is capable of a lot of things. By humans, for humans.

#### Written by mindful and careful programmers

The codebase is written mindfully by human developers (yes, even these words) committed at exploiting AI's capability in teaching to its saturation, establishing a system where there is small margin for error and little chance for AI hallucination. The codebase is human-written and AI-assisted coding is disclosed. We believe AI should only be used in where it only must be.

#### Open-source

Mindful.ly's source code is available to the public.

#### Bring your own CLI agent

Mindful.ly runs on your agent subscription at no extra cost.

#### Local-first

No data leaves your machine (not including extra-application data). Every workspace and uploaded resource remains local. Mindful.ly does not collect and sell your data.

#### AI in where AI should be

Artificial intelligence, particularly large language models, are touted to displace human talents from their jobs. Mindful.ly is a resistance against that idea. While not taking the position on AI's capability on superseding human workers, regardless, no human worker must feel threatened about their job security to AI.

Mindful.ly is an application of AI in AI must only be: to enhance the human person.
